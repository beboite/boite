# UI frames, 2026-09-30

`bench/ui-frames.ts` on the fake client, headless Chrome with software
compositing (`--disable-gpu --disable-software-rasterizer --disable-3d-apis`),
in a 16-thread Linux container that other jobs shared. "Base" is `3fb0576`,
measured with `--ui <extracted base>/packages/ui`; "after" is this branch. Each
scenario measures three seconds of `requestAnimationFrame`. The main-thread
figure is Chrome's `TaskDuration` per second, and per 1,000 px scrolled for the
scroll scenarios.

```sh
bun bench/ui-frames.ts --ui <base>/packages/ui
bun bench/ui-frames.ts
bun bench/ui-frames.ts --runs 3 --only "long thread scroll,scroll while streaming,phone long scroll"
bun bench/ui-frames.ts --cpu 4 --only "palette over stream,onboarding tour,streaming answer,activity panel over stream"
bun bench/ui-frames.ts --size 1920x1080@1.5 --only "palette over stream,onboarding tour,long thread scroll,scroll under activity"
```

## Window-wide blur

| Scenario | Size, CPU | Base fps | After fps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Command palette over a streaming answer | 1280 x 890, x1 | 11.7 | 60.0 |
| Onboarding tour, scene replaying | 1280 x 890, x1 | 11.5 | 59.7 |
| Command palette over a streaming answer | 1280 x 890, x4 | 11.5 | 60.0 |
| Onboarding tour, scene replaying | 1280 x 890, x4 | 11.7 | 60.0 |
| Command palette over a streaming answer | 1920 x 1080 at 1.5, x1 | 6.2 | 60.0 |
| Onboarding tour, scene replaying | 1920 x 1080 at 1.5, x1 | 10.8 | 60.0 |

The same base frames with backdrop filters switched off drew 58 fps for the
palette and 50 to 60 fps for the tour. The welcome/agents tour scenes at
1280 x 890 measured 9 to 13 fps with blur and 50 to 60 fps without it on
2026-09-30, as recorded by the onboarding guide.

## Scrolling, three runs each, 1280 x 890, CPU x1

Measured last, on the final head, while the container was less loaded than for
the tables above.

| Scenario | Base fps | After fps | Base main ms / 1,000 px | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wheel up a 400-message thread | 60.0 | 60.0 | 340 | 310 |
| The same while an answer streams | 60.0 | 60.0 | 278 | 264 |
| Phone, 390 x 844 at 2 | 55.9 | 57.9 | 441 | 423 |

An earlier head also read the reading anchor one frame after a scroll, which
took the first row to 392 ms and was dropped: a tap right after a scroll then
restored the next message (`tests/e2e/mobile.test.ts`).

## Unchanged, 60 fps before and after

Idle thread, streaming answer, the activity panel (401 px, 16 px blur) over a
streaming answer at CPU x1 and x4, phone streaming.

## Limits

- A GPU. WebView2 with acceleration rasterizes on the GPU; these numbers are
  its worst case, not its usual one.
- Flick scrolling on a CPU four times slower stays main-thread bound at about
  20 to 30 fps, before and after, mounting the messages the window reaches.
  Runs on that setting varied by a third from one to the next under the
  container's other load.
- At 1920 x 1080 at 1.5, scrolling varied from 34 to 58 fps between runs with
  and without the remaining small blurs alike: software rasterizing the page
  bounds it there, not the blur.
