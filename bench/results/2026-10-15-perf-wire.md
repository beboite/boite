# Wire fan-out and live attachments, 2026-10-15

Linux LXC, Ryzen 7 5825U shared with other jobs, Bun 1.4.2. Each command ran
3 times back to back on `origin/main` (3e535df3) and then on the branch, with
the same `bench/fanout.ts`. Each run reports a median over 30 emits; the table
gives the median of the 3 runs.

```sh
nice -n 10 bun bench/fanout.ts
```

## Emit time of a 1 MiB tool `message.part`

Log text with quotes, tabs and a non-ASCII letter, sent to loopback sockets
that opened the thread without compaction, timed around the synchronous
`bus.emit`.

| Sockets | Before (ms) | After (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5.54 | 5.60 |
| 2 | 9.47 | 6.71 |
| 4 | 18.98 | 9.08 |

Runs before: 1 socket 5.54, 6.15, 4.64; 2 sockets 10.83, 8.98, 9.47;
4 sockets 20.21, 16.32, 18.98. Runs after: 1 socket 5.70, 5.02, 5.60;
2 sockets 6.15, 6.71, 6.83; 4 sockets 8.35, 10.20, 9.08. What is left per
extra socket is Bun's `send` copying the frame.

## Bytes of a live prompt with a 1 MiB picture

A remote window, connected through the counting relay of `bench/lib/wire.ts`
with a non-loopback Host and opened as the UI opens a thread (`compactTools`,
`compactToolParts`, `compactFiles`, `compactImages`), sends a prompt with a
1 MiB picture. Down bytes are counted from the request until its own
`message.started` arrives, deflate included.

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Down bytes | 1,057,351 | 4,273 |
| Picture in the event | whole | deferred |

Runs before: 1,057,351, 1,057,187, 1,057,355. Runs after: 4,267, 4,273, 4,287.

## Delta pacing

Merged text is no longer serialized to be counted until its bound nears the
16 MiB frame limit. Unmeasured: the change removes one or two
`JSON.stringify` calls per paced delta on each remote socket, and
`packages/core/test/wire.test.ts` checks that the flush still falls at the
exact limit.
